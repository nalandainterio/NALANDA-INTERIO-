01What we do

Everything a kitchen needs. Nothing it doesn't.

From the first measurement to the last handle — one team, no subcontractors, no surprises.

01 Modular kitchens

L-shaped, U-shaped, island and parallel layouts — carcasses in BWP-grade plywood, soft-close hardware throughout, and finishes from laminate to high-gloss acrylic.

BWP plywood · Soft-close hardware · Acrylic & laminate finishes

02 Kitchen renovation

We strip, re-route and rebuild existing kitchens — plumbing, electrical and civil work handled end-to-end, with your family back in the kitchen in under three weeks.

Civil & plumbing · Electrical rework · 3-week turnaround

03 Countertops & backsplash

Quartz, granite and solid-surface counters cut to your cooking habits, paired with easy-clean backsplashes and honest advice on what will last in an Indian kitchen.

Quartz & granite · Seamless joins · Heat & stain resistant

04 Storage & tall units

Pantry pull-outs, corner carousels, bottle units and overhead lofts — every inch planned around what your family actually owns and uses daily.

Tall pantries · Corner carousels · Under-sink organisation

05 Lighting & utilities

Task lighting under the counters, chimney and hob selection, water-purifier and bin integration — the quiet details that make a kitchen genuinely work.

Under-cabinet lighting · Chimney & hob · Appliance integration
